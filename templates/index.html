Multi-Method XAI Comparison Triangulation

Comparing Grad-CAM, LIME, and SHAP ensures no single explanation method misleads clinical interpretation.

Grad-CAM: White-box internal feature gradients. Instant computation. Shows receptive field activation.
LIME: Model-agnostic local perturbations. Identifies which superpixel segments support vs oppose the verdict.
SHAP: Axiomatic game theory. Measures marginal contribution of each tissue segment towards/against tumor likelihood.
Triangulation Consensus: When all 3 methods highlight overlapping renal parenchyma regions, confidence in model focus is substantially heightened.
